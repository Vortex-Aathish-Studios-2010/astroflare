Research explorer (Lunar habitat demo, NASA research) searches NASA records in your browser. Mission screening sends your answers to the assessment engine on this site’s server.

    What this is and is not. Astroflare screens a hypothetical scenario you describe. It is not connected to any spacecraft or sensor and cannot detect or report a real event. The output is a preliminary screening: it has not been validated against experiments or reviewed by a qualified safety authority, and it is not a safety determination.

    How this site runs. AstroFlare is one service on Render: this web interface, a Python assessment engine and an optional AI explanation service (FreeLLMAPI using Google Gemini models), all in the same container. Results come from fixed rules and cited NASA sources. AI text is optional, checked and labelled.

    Sources and credits. Experiment records: NASA Physical Sciences Informatics (BASS-II, PSI-25; Saffire I, PSI-98; Saffire II, PSI-99). Literature: NASA Technical Reports Server.
